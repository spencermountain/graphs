Converted from the 2019 browserify/js original in public/2019/nhl-history (four `somehow` charts read out of assets/bundle.js).
Approximations: the used parts of the `somehow` v0.0.10 lib (linear scales, monotone-x curves from d3-shape, axes, rect/line/midArea/text/annotation) are hand-ported in somehow.js; dates parse as UTC instead of spacetime-local (sub-pixel difference on an 84-year axis); "today" is a fixed epoch at prerender, updated to Date.now() on mount; unused data/teams.js and scripts/fetch.js were not copied; page shell/typography follows this repo's card style instead of spencer.min.css.
