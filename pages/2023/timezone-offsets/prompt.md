Converted from the 2023 svelte/js original in public/2023/timezone-offsets. The lib/compute chain (prepYear/by-year/calculate/_lib) was ported into one co-located getYear.js; zonefile/patterns/misc data copied verbatim. Faithful quirks kept: zones with ad-hoc misc epochs (Casablanca, Tehran, Jerusalem…) drop out in those years, the current dot only tracks the spring change (it stays at the summer offset until the year rolls over), and un-pausing runs faster (200ms) than the initial 500ms tick.
