Converted from the 2018 browserify/somehow original in public/2018/sports-by-city.
Notes: the `somehow` v0.0.8 svg engine (470x200 'widescreen' chart, pixel-from-bottom y coords, truncating linear x-scale, 12 month ticks) was reimplemented as inline svg in the template — 2-point curveMonotoneX paths became `<line>` elements (identical for straight segments), and `<g transform>`+tspan text became `<text>` at the equivalent baselines. Season dates are the 2018 ones applied to the current year, as the original did; the dotted today-line is computed on mount. The one-line gray subtitle was added for the standard header shell (the original had only a title).
